Aurora — Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Aurora is an NYC Subway and NJ PATH navigation app. Privacy summary: your data never leaves your phone.

What Aurora accesses and why

  • Location (including background): used only during an active trip to count down stops and alert you at your destination. Processed entirely on-device. Never stored beyond the trip, never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Motion & accelerometer: used during an active trip to detect station stops in tunnels where GPS is unavailable. Processed on-device, never stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: used to alert you when it's time to get off.

Network requests

Aurora fetches public real-time train arrival feeds directly from the MTA (api-endpoint.mta.info) and the Port Authority (panynj.gov). These requests contain no personal information, no identifiers, and no location data.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising.
  • No data collection, sale, or sharing of any kind.

Purchases

Aurora Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
